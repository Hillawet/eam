OUR STORY ON THE WATER

Who we are

The Crocoplant Farm is located in Caribbean Bay, Kariba, Zimbabwe. Mr Thando Mhlathuzi opened as a boat rental company in 2002 and later became Asset Rental Company. It has grown to become one of the biggest boat rental and accommodation providers in Kariba.

The company has different asset-rental sections. The Luxury Boats Section offers luxury boat rentals, Car Rental, Heavy Machinery with a team of four employees serving about ninety (90) customers per week. The Fishing Boats Section offers fishing boat rentals and serves more than two hundred (200) customers per week.

What began as two modest vessels at Harbor Bay Marina has grown into one of the region’s most respected and beloved charter operations.

Our name comes from our roots in big game fishing — a tradition that still beats at the heart of everything we do. Today, alongside our world-class fishing charters, we offer an expansive range of luxury cruises, private events, corporate charters, and coastal adventures.

Every boat we operate, every crew member we hire, and every charter we run reflects our founding belief: that time on the water should be extraordinary.
