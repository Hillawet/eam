About Croco Plant

Built around reliable assets and practical support.

Croco Plant helps businesses, families and project teams access the vehicles, boats, equipment and asset support they need across Lake Kariba and surrounding areas. Our model is simple: dependable availability, transparent communication and flexible rental terms.

Who we are

The company has different asset-rental sections. The Luxury Boats Section offers luxury boat rentals, Car Rental, Heavy Machinery with a team of four employees serving about ninety (90) customers per week. The Fishing Boats Section offers fishing boat rentals and serves more than two hundred (200) customers per week.
